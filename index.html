About 876

Caribbean cooking, made fresh on Ecclesall Road.

876 Kitchen brings the lively flavours of the Caribbean to Sheffield — jerk, curry, oxtail, rice and peas — cooked with traditional methods, fresh ingredients, and the kind of warm welcome you'd expect at someone's kitchen table.

The name is a nod home: 876 is the calling code of Jamaica. Every platter on the menu carries that line back to the islands — the spice cupboards, the slow-cooked stews, the Sunday lunches.

Fresh, local, traditional. Ingredients sourced as locally as Caribbean cooking allows, prepped fresh each day.

Fully halal. The whole kitchen, top to bottom.

Vegan options. Ackee, plantain, rice and peas, callaloo — the island has always known how to feed a plant-based plate.

Dine in or takeaway. Eat in with us on Ecclesall Road, or order through Deliveroo, Uber Eats or Just Eat.
